About

A business student in Mumbai, with one foot in a family trade and the other in digital work.

I'm Vishesh, an undergraduate pursuing two BBA programmes at the same time: Digital Business and Entrepreneurship at IIM Bangalore, and Digital Business at K. P. B. Hinduja College of Commerce in Mumbai.

Family jewellery business

I take an active part in my family's jewellery business. I help with day-to-day operations and talk with customers to understand what they're looking for, so their buying experience feels personal.

It has given me a working knowledge of the trade, from the product range to how much depends on customer trust and brand reputation. That's a big part of why I'm drawn to marketing and brand building.

Web and digital

I like building things on the web. In 2024 I won first place at an inter-school web development competition with a website I built.

Alongside that I work with video editing, presentation design, content planning and generative AI tools, which I use through prompt engineering.

Leadership at Tremor

At Tremor, Hinduja College's fest, I'm Vice Head of the Technicals department and a member of the Public Relations team.

I co-lead the department with the Head of Department: delegating tasks, tracking timelines, resolving issues, and coordinating with the other organising teams so events run on schedule.
